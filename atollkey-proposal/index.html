An Island Built for
Unhurried Mornings

On Dhigurah — a single-village island in the South Ari Atoll known for its resident whale sharks and one of the longest natural beaches in the Maldives — Atoll Key offers a quieter kind of luxury. Minimalist local architecture, panoramic ocean views from every room, and a two-hour speedboat (or 20-minute flight to Maamigili) from Malé.

Things To Do on Dhigurah

Whale Shark Snorkeling

Night Dives
